📜 Tournament Rules

GEC FFIC CUP — SRGEC | Free Fire BR 2026

1. Eligibility

  • Each team must have exactly 4 playing members.
  • At least 1 of the 4 playing members must be an SRGEC student — verified via college ID upload in the registration form.
  • 1 substitute is allowed per team. The substitute does NOT need to be from SRGEC.
  • The substitute may be used only between matches, not mid-match.
  • A player cannot register for or play with more than one team.

2. Match Format

  • Mode: Battle Royale (BR) — Squad (4 players).
  • Map: Bermuda ONLY.
  • 12 teams per lobby (one group per match day).
  • Group Stage: each team plays 2 matches. Combined points decide group standings.
  • Top 6 from Group A + top 6 from Group B advance to the Grand Final.

4. Equipment & In-Game Settings

  • Character skills: ALLOWED.
  • Gun attributes: BANNED (attribute-granted stat boosts must be disabled).
  • Only mobile devices allowed — no emulators, tablets, or PC.
  • Using any hack, mod, or third-party tool = immediate disqualification.
  • Gloo walls, medkits, and standard items are allowed.
  • Teaming with other squads = disqualification of both teams.

5. Match Conduct

  • Join the Custom Room using the ID and password posted in the WhatsApp group.
  • All players must be in the room 5 minutes before the match starts.
  • Late teams (>2 minutes after start) may forfeit that match.
  • Do not drop, restart, or leave the match — disconnection = your responsibility.
  • After every match, every team must submit a full-screen screenshot showing kills and ranking to the WhatsApp group.

6. Proof & Disputes

  • Screenshots are the only accepted proof for kills, placement, and scoring issues.
  • Submit disputes within 10 minutes after match end — no disputes after the next match begins.
  • Organizer's decision is final.

7. Entry Fee & Prizes

  • Entry fee: ₹200 per team — payable via UPI (QR on the home page).
  • Entry confirmed only after the organizers verify payment.
  • Prizes: 1st ₹1500, 2nd ₹1000, 3rd ₹800, Most Kills ₹500.
⚠️ Disqualification is immediate for: cheating, hacking, emulator use, teaming, abusive behavior, or using a non-registered player. Entry fee is non-refundable in case of disqualification.

8. Code of Conduct

  • Respect all players, organizers, and moderators.
  • No toxic chat, abuse, or harassment — offline or in-game.
  • Organizers reserve the right to update rules; changes will be announced in the WhatsApp group.
  • Organizer's decision in all matters is final.
